## Theory

### What is LAN Scanning?
LAN (Local Area Network) scanning is the process of discovering devices connected within a local network. It helps identify active hosts, open ports, and available services.

LAN scanning is commonly used in cybersecurity for:
- Network monitoring
- Vulnerability assessment
- Detecting unauthorized devices
